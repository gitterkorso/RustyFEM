# Privacy policy

Effective date: 5 November 2026

RustyFEM is made by Nick Westin. This policy covers the RustyFEM apps for iOS,
iPadOS, macOS, Windows and Linux.

**RustyFEM does not collect, store on any server, sell or share your personal
data.** It has no user accounts, advertising, analytics or crash reporting, and
it does not track you.

## Data on your device

Everything you work with stays on your device:

- **Models and results.** Projects and other files are saved only where you
  choose to save them. Files you open are read only so that the app can work
  with them.
- **Settings.** Preferences such as units and the state of the interface, and
  the settings of the MCP server including its access token, are stored locally
  by the app. Deleting the app on iOS and iPadOS removes them.

The developer has no access to any of this data.

## Network use

RustyFEM does not connect to the internet by itself.

The optional MCP server lets AI assistants and other tools that you connect
work with the app. It is off until you turn it on or set up a connection to it,
listens only on your own device by default and requires an access token. If you
turn on "Allow from network", it also accepts connections from other devices on
your local network, and on iOS and iPadOS the system asks for your permission
first.

A tool you connect can read and change the model that is open in the app, see
its results, and open and save files that the app has access to. What that tool
does with the data is covered by its own terms and privacy policy.

## App stores and third parties

RustyFEM contains no third-party services that receive your data. If you
download the app from Apple's App Store, Apple may process data about the
download and may provide the developer with anonymous, aggregated statistics
under its own privacy policy. The same applies to GitHub when you download a
release or open an issue there.

## Children

RustyFEM is not aimed at children and does not knowingly collect data from
anyone, including children.

## Changes

Changes to this policy are published in this file, and the history of the file
is public. The effective date above is updated when the policy changes.
